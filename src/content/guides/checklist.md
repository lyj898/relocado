Three dates drive everything else: your last working day, the day you hand back your home, and your flight. Fix those first, then work backwards. The [leaving Singapore planner](/leaving-singapore/planner/) does the arithmetic and puts each task below on a calendar date.

The order matters more than the speed. Lease notice and mover surveys have the longest lead times, and much of what follows depends on them.

## Three months out: read the fine print

- **Confirm your last working day in writing.** It's the date your employer puts on the IR21 tax clearance form, and [IRAS's notes on the form](https://www.iras.gov.sg/docs/default-source/uploadedfiles/pdf/explanatorynotestoformir21.pdf) say it isn't the day your pass is cancelled. Your employer [must file at least one month before it](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/individual-income-tax/employers/tax-clearance-for-foreign-spr-employees-(ir21)/getting-tax-clearance-a-step-by-step-guide), and from the day it knows you're leaving it has to withhold all money due to you. The [IR21 guide](/leaving-singapore/ir21-tax-clearance/) explains what that means for your final pay.
- **Read your tenancy agreement.** Look for a diplomatic clause: how many months must pass before you can use it, how much notice it needs, and what proof. In [CEA's recommended template](https://isomer-user-content.by.gov.sg/71/60052678-1592-4904-b443-3269a655e0de/Tenancy%20Agreement%20Template%20for%20Private%20Residential%20Property.pdf), notice has to come with documentary evidence, such as a letter from your employer. The [diplomatic clause guide](/leaving-singapore/diplomatic-clause/) covers the rest.
- **Taking a pet?** Check your destination's import rules now, because they set the timeline. Pet relocation specialists are listed in the [directory](/directory/#pet-relocation).
- **Giving up PR status?** [ICA](https://www.ica.gov.sg/enter-transit-depart/more-information/for-permanent-residents/renunciation-of-permanent-residence) takes up to four weeks to process a renunciation, and advises staying in Singapore until it's complete, so leave room for that.
- **Children in school?** Check each school's terms for how much notice it needs before a withdrawal.

## Eight to six weeks out: decide what goes

- **Sort before the survey.** Under [FIDI's guidelines](https://www.fidi.org/sites/default/files/public/2021-01/FIDI%20Professional%20Cooperation%20Guidelines_2020.pdf) for international movers, the surveyor records what you show or describe, and the quote follows the survey. Decide what you're shipping, selling, storing and throwing away first, using [ship, sell, store or throw away](/leaving-singapore/ship-sell-store-or-dispose/). Then invite two or three movers from the [directory](/directory/#international-movers).
- **Give notice**, if you're using a diplomatic clause. Two months is common, but your agreement sets the number. Serve it the way the agreement says and keep a copy.
- **Start selling.** List the things you won't miss first, and keep the bed, fridge and washing machine until the final days.
- **Book the mover and your packing date**, and ask for its list of items it won't take. [What movers won't ship](/leaving-singapore/what-movers-wont-ship/) explains the common ones, and what to do with them here.
- **Car, telco and other contracts.** Find out each one's termination terms now. The [last two weeks guide](/leaving-singapore/last-two-weeks/) covers selling or deregistering a car.

## One month out: paperwork and bookings

- **Check the IR21 has been filed.** IRAS suggests asking your employer.
- **Set up your bank account for refunds.** IRAS pays refunds electronically only. It [advises leavers](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/individual-income-tax/employers/tax-clearance-for-foreign-spr-employees-(ir21)/refunds) to keep an active Singapore-dollar account and link it to their FIN (foreign identification number) through PayNow, at least 30 days before departure.
- **Book appliance collections.** ALBA, the e-waste recycler licensed by NEA, [collects large appliances](https://alba-ewaste.sg/doorstep-collection/) such as fridges and washing machines from homes for free, but on weekdays only, booked 7 to 14 working days ahead.
- **Don't count on the town council.** In an HDB flat, free bulky-item removal is capped at three items a month at the town councils we checked, and [Aljunied-Hougang's](https://www.ahtc.sg/bulky-item-removal/) excludes house-moving, as do the other two.
- **Sort out where you'll sleep.** If the flat has to be empty before your flight, [URA](https://www.ura.gov.sg/guidelines/property-and-business-owners/property/short-term-accommodation/) notes that hotels have no minimum stay and serviced apartments have a seven-day minimum.

## Two weeks out: packing day and the final bookings

- **Packing day.** Before the packers arrive, set aside everything that flies with you: passports, pass cards, tenancy and employment documents, medication, valuables and laptops.
- **Book the final clear-out.** Whatever didn't sell, find a new home or fit in the container still has to leave before handover. Junk to Clear, the team behind Relocado, [clears anything from a few items to a whole flat](https://junktoclear.com.sg/services/residential-waste-disposal-singapore/), seven days a week.
- **Keeping a few things in Singapore?** HomeToMoved, run by the same team as Relocado, matches you with vetted movers for [single items](https://hometomoved.com/moving/single-item-moving/), such as the things going to a friend's place or into storage.
- **Book the move-out clean and the aircon service.** CEA's template expects the flat thoroughly cleaned at handover, with receipts for air-conditioner servicing, which it requires at least every three months. Your agreement may say more.

The [last two weeks guide](/leaving-singapore/last-two-weeks/) walks through this stretch in more detail.

## The final week: clear, clean, hand back

- **Clear, then clean.** Once the flat is empty, the clean can reach every cabinet and corner.
- **Keep the power and water on for the joint inspection.** CEA's template says the tenant shouldn't close the utilities account before then, and has the landlord apply to close it afterwards. Agree the arrangement with your landlord.
- **Return every key and access card.** Under CEA's template, the tenancy doesn't end until you do, and rent runs until then.
- **Take dated photos** of each room at the handover, and keep them with your inventory list.
- **Your pass.** Your employer must [cancel your work pass](https://www.mom.gov.sg/passes-and-permits/employment-pass/cancel-a-pass) within a week after your last day of notice, and your family's passes are cancelled with it. If you're staying on for a while, ask HR to request a Short-Term Visit Pass. The [HR departure checklist](/for-hr-teams/departure-checklist/) sets out what your employer has to do.

## After you land

- **Keep an eye on your tax account.** If the money your employer withheld doesn't cover your tax, [IRAS says](https://www.iras.gov.sg/taxes/individual-income-tax/employees/scenario-based-faqs-for-working-in-singapore-and-abroad/tax-clearance-for-non-singapore-citizen-employees) the balance is due within seven days of your Statement of Account.
- **Follow up the deposit.** Agree before you leave how it will reach you, and keep that bank account open until it has.
- **If you gave up PR status**, you can close your CPF account and [transfer your savings](https://www.cpf.gov.sg/member/account-services/cpf-asset-management/on-leaving-singapore) once the renunciation is complete. If you don't, CPF Board closes it the following month and the savings stop earning CPF interest.
